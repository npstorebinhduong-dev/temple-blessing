Thai Dawn — original instrumental for Ganesha LIVE

This composition and recording were created for this project using the
deterministic synthesizer in scripts/render-thai-dawn.py. No third-party music,
sample recording, pre-existing melody, chant, or voice recording is included.

The recording may be used, copied, modified, redistributed, and played in
commercial livestreams under the project's MIT license (see LICENSE at the
repository root). Retain this notice when redistributing the recording.

The synthesized colors are inspired by khim, ranat, khlui and light ching/gong
sounds. This is a new relaxation instrumental, not an authentic recording of
Thai Buddhist liturgy or a claim of traditional ceremonial repertoire.

Duration: 128 seconds. The rendered note tails and room reflections wrap
across the boundary for continuous replay. Runtime playback uses Web Audio
buffer looping; no Internet service is required to play the bundled music.
